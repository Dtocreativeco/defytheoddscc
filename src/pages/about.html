About

Where you start doesn't have to determine where you finish.

The story

Why "Defy The Odds"

"Statistically, I wasn't supposed to graduate high school, let alone build a business."

Defy The Odds Creative Co. is named for the choice Jesse Walker Jr. made not to be a statistic. Now he helps other people and businesses tell their own stories.

That belief, that where you start doesn't have to determine where you finish, came from Jesse's own path. He chose a different road than the one expected of him, pursued his education and started a business young, with faith at the center of it all.

Today that same mindset drives the work we do for our clients. Plenty of great local businesses never get the attention they deserve, not because the work isn't good, but because their marketing doesn't show it. We exist to change that.

What we do

We create moments that move people.

We help growing businesses across the Piedmont Triad show up online the way they deserve to, with content that gets seen, builds trust and brings customers through the door.

We handle the strategy, content creation, video and social media, so you get consistent, professional marketing without spending your own time behind a camera or a content calendar. Every project is built to move your business forward, not just fill a feed.

"Our business is better than our marketing makes it look."

What we hear most

Almost every business owner we talk to says some version of this. They're great at what they do. They just don't have the time, strategy or team to show it.

So we act as that team. We bring the strategy and the creative, we handle the execution, and we keep you in the loop without adding to your plate.

How we work

What you can expect from us

  • A partner, not a vendor. We bring ideas and strategy to the table, not just a to do list.
  • Focus. We do four things and we do them well, instead of trying to offer every marketing service out there.
  • Honesty. Clear recommendations, clear pricing and no pressure to buy what you don't need.
  • Local roots. We live and work in the Triad, and we know the business community here.
