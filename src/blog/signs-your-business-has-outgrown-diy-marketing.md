<p>Most businesses start out doing their own marketing. The owner posts when they can, snaps photos on their phone and figures it out along the way. That works for a while. But at some point, the business grows and the marketing doesn't keep up. Here are five signs you've reached that point.</p>

<h2>1. Your business is better than your marketing makes it look</h2>

<p>This is the one we hear most. Your customers love you, your work is great, but your online presence doesn't show it. When people compare you to competitors online, they're judging you by what they see, not by what you actually do.</p>

<h2>2. Posting only happens when someone remembers</h2>

<p>If your social media goes quiet every time things get busy, it's a sign marketing has become an afterthought. Inconsistent posting makes a business look less active than it really is.</p>

<h2>3. You're not sure what's working</h2>

<p>If you can't say which posts or campaigns actually brought in customers, you're guessing. Good marketing has a plan and a way to measure it.</p>

<h2>4. Marketing is eating your time</h2>

<p>Every hour you spend figuring out captions or editing video is an hour you're not spending with customers or running the business. At a certain size, that trade no longer makes sense.</p>

<h2>5. You have big moments with no plan</h2>

<p>A grand opening, a new service or a big event deserves more than a post the day before. If important moments keep sneaking up on you, it's time for a real campaign plan.</p>
