<p>Short form video is one of the best ways for a local business to get noticed right now. Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts let people see your team, your space and your work in less than a minute. The good news is you don't need a big production to make them work. Here are seven ideas that work well for service businesses across the Triad.</p>

<h2>1. A day in the life</h2>

<p>Show what a normal day looks like: opening up, prepping, working with customers, wrapping up. People love seeing the real side of a business, and it makes you feel familiar before they ever walk in.</p>

<h2>2. Before and after</h2>

<p>If your work has a visible result, show it. Before and after clips are simple, satisfying and easy to understand without any explanation.</p>

<h2>3. Meet the team</h2>

<p>Introduce one person at a time with a quick clip about what they do and something fun about them. Customers trust businesses when they know the people behind them.</p>

<h2>4. Answer a common question</h2>

<p>Take a question you hear all the time and answer it in 30 seconds. This builds authority and gives people a reason to follow you.</p>

<h2>5. Customer story</h2>

<p>A short clip of a happy customer talking about their experience is one of the strongest pieces of content you can post. Always ask permission first.</p>

<h2>6. Behind the process</h2>

<p>Show how something gets done: how a product is made, how a service is delivered or how you prepare for a big job. Process content makes your work feel valuable.</p>

<h2>7. Local moments</h2>

<p>Film your business at local events, community days and partnerships around town. It shows you're part of the community, and local audiences love seeing places they recognize.</p>
