<p>It's one of the first questions we hear from business owners in Greensboro, Reidsville and across the Triad: how often should we actually be posting? The honest answer is that consistency matters more than volume. A business that posts three solid times a week, every week, will almost always beat one that posts every day for two weeks and then disappears for a month.</p>

<h2>A realistic starting point</h2>

<p>For most local service businesses, a good baseline looks like this: three to four feed posts or Reels a week on Instagram and Facebook, a few stories during the week to show what's happening day to day, one or two posts a week on LinkedIn if your customers are other businesses, and one Google Business Profile post a week.</p>

<p>That's enough to stay visible and show that you're active, without creating so much work that it falls apart the first busy week.</p>

<h2>Why consistency beats volume</h2>

<p>When someone finds your business, they usually check your social media before they call. If the last post is from months ago, they wonder if you're still open, still busy or still care. A steady rhythm of posts tells them you're active and trustworthy.</p>

<p>Consistency also helps you on the platforms themselves. Accounts that post regularly tend to build more engagement over time, and engagement is what keeps your content showing up for the people who already follow you.</p>

<h2>What to post</h2>

<p>You don't need a new idea every day. Most strong local business accounts rotate through a handful of content types: behind the scenes looks at your team and process, finished work and customer results, quick tips that answer common questions, introductions to the people on your team, customer stories and testimonials, and timely updates like events, specials and new services.</p>

<p>Short form video is especially powerful right now. A 20 to 45 second clip of your team at work often reaches more people than a polished graphic.</p>

<h2>How to keep it going</h2>

<p>The businesses that stay consistent usually plan ahead. They batch content in one session instead of scrambling every morning, they keep a simple monthly calendar, and they schedule posts in advance.</p>
